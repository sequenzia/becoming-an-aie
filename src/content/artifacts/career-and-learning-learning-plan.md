Written 2026-09-29. Owner: Dana (backend engineer, payments team)

## Goal
Become a certified AI engineer by March 2027 and move into a Staff AI
Engineer role. Completing this plan guarantees I am job-ready.

## What I will master
1. Memorize the context window, price, and best use of every Larkspur
   and Brightline model (lark-4-mini, lark-4, bright-pro-2, bright-flash-2).
2. Learn all 14 node types in Loomflow 2.3 and build every tutorial graph.
3. Learn Driftcode CLI 4.1 power commands: /turbo, /swarm, and the "eco"
   reasoning level for cheap runs.
4. Get the Loomflow Certified Agent Developer badge (Level 2).
5. Collect the 50 best prompts from the "Prompt Drop" newsletter and
   learn the phrase that stops lark-4-mini from hallucinating.
6. Learn RAG by setting up Vectorbin with default settings.
7. Watch 40 hours of conference talks.

## Schedule
October: items 1 and 3. November: item 2. December: item 4.
January: items 5 and 6. February: item 7. March: apply for roles.

## How I will track progress
- Tutorials completed (target 30)
- Badge earned
- Hours watched
- Models memorized

## Not doing
- Reading papers (too academic)
- Evals (I will learn them on the job)
