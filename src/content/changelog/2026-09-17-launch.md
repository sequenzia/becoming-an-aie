The site is planned to open on the day of the talk. This entry records the Phase 0 commit, the first version of the site that runs. It carries the talk's thesis, verbatim: "Using AI makes you an AI-enabled software engineer." "Engineering systems that depend on AI makes you an AI engineer."

What is live at the Phase 0 commit:

- The landing page, with the six-area map and the fourteen planned modules listed by name.
- The notify-me form. Addresses are stored unconfirmed. No mail goes out until a provider is chosen and confirmed opt-in is enabled. The privacy notice says what is stored, why, and how to have it removed.
- The module catalog, with every module listed as planned.
- The map page and this changelog.

What comes next: Orientation, then Foundations and the six area modules, then the self-assessment and the electives. Each module opens when it passes the content check. Every change to the modules, the artifacts, and the site is recorded here, newest first.
