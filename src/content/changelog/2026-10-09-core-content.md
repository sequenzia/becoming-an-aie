This entry records the Phase 2 commit. Foundations and the six area modules are published. Each passed the content check with every rule applied. Reading times leave out the workshop and the failure exercise.

What opened:

- Foundations, about 60 minutes. A collapsed mathematical reference is optional reading on top of that.
- Models, 52 minutes.
- Context and knowledge, 51 minutes.
- Tools and extensibility, 50 minutes.
- Orchestration, 50 minutes.
- Verification and evals, 52 minutes.
- Operating it, 55 minutes.

Each module has a workshop, a failure exercise that plants the area's pitfall, a self-check of twelve questions, and a dated source list. Vendor and tool facts were checked on 2026-10-09 and are stated as true on that day. Where a source could not be read again, its note says so and keeps the earlier date.

Artifacts: 25 new cards. 24 are synthetic. They were written for the exercises, the failures in them are planted on purpose, and they use fictional companies, vendors, and model ids. None is a measured result. One is public: an excerpt of Anthropic's postmortem of 2025-09-17, "A postmortem of three recent issues", placed in Operating it with its source link. Nothing in this release was captured from the author's sessions, so no captured artifact needed a review date.

Also changed:

- Orientation now links to each area module where it names the areas, the pitfalls, and where to go next. One sentence was corrected: not every area module heads its owner section "When you are the owner".
- The Phase 1 fixture trace, `models-fixture-trace`, was removed. No published page ever showed it.
- The Models self-check grew from the fixture's six questions to twelve. The first six kept their ids, and some of their wording changed.

Reading any module needs no account. Signing in saves your workshop and failure responses, keeps your self-check results, and shows your progress in the catalog.

What comes next: the self-assessment, the guided plan, and the first electives and labs. The Models optional lab is part of that release.
