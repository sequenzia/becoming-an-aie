## Transfer connection

This module is planned. It is not yet written. This page holds its place in the catalog until it is.

Testing discipline expands into statistical judgment about populations and repeated measurements. Debugging and data modeling become tools for understanding what a model learns. The module will say what stays the same and what changes.

## Topics and learning outcomes

<Outcomes />

The module draws on book chapters 4, 5, and 6: experimental thinking and uncertainty, what a model learns and why generalization is not training success, and how foundation models work and fail. Each concept will be introduced with the decision it affects later and a link to the area module where it is used. A short mathematical reference will sit in a collapsible section. Learners with a machine learning background can skip this module. The catalog marks it as recommended before Verification and evals, not required.
