## How the assessment works

This module is planned. It is not yet written. This page holds its place in the catalog until it is.

The assessment walks through the six areas in talk order. For each area it presents the transfer items and the new competencies and asks for a rating on a four-point scale: Not yet, Aware, Practiced, Confident. Three context questions come with it: your current role, the AI feature you are closest to at work, and whether you own a model-dependent system today. The plan ranks the areas by gap and applies the talk's four-step roadmap to your context.

## The six areas

Models, Context and knowledge, Tools and extensibility, Orchestration, Verification and evals, Operating it. Each area's items are listed in this module's frontmatter, so the assessment and this page cannot drift apart.

## After the plan

The plan is saved to your account, editable, and exportable as Markdown. A retake keeps the previous plan as a dated version. Completing the assessment does not require completing every area module; the plan names the ones you have not finished. An optional feedback form closes the module.
